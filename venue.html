Music Bingo
Main Course
Music Bingo is Belladonna's signature twist on a classic — instead of numbers, guests race to mark off songs as we play them. It's fast, it's loud, and it keeps the whole room engaged from the first track to the last. We bring themed bingo cards, a full-service host, and a custom playlist built around whatever energy your venue wants to bring. From 90s throwbacks to country nights to holiday specials, every Music Bingo night is built to fill seats and drive sales at the bar.
