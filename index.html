CSV Format Guide

Your CSV should have the following columns. The Name and Shows columns are required.

Name, Type, Theme, Shows
"Monsters Magic & Melodrama Trivia", "Trivia", "Spooky / Gothic", "Vampire Diaries | Buffy | The Originals | True Blood"
"Teen Shows Trivia", "Trivia", "Youth TV", "Vampire Diaries | Gilmore Girls | Dawsons Creek"
"Witchcore Trivia", "Trivia", "Witches & Magic", "Sabrina | Charmed | Vampire Diaries | The Craft"

Separate multiple shows with a pipe | character. Theme is optional but recommended for filtering.
