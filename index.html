CSV Format Guide

Two formats are supported — export directly from your spreadsheet, no reformatting needed.

Trivia / Standard format

Category, Sub-Category, Themes/Keywords, Key Examples/Notes
"FANTASY / SUPERNATURAL", "Witchcore Trivia", "Witchcraft, occult", "Charmed, Sabrina, The Craft"

Bingo format

Category, Title, Themes/Keywords
"BINGO", "Witchcore Bingo", "Witchcore"
"BINGO", "90s Metal Bingo", "90s, Metal"

Bingo categories are hidden by default in the library — toggle "Show Bingo" near the search bar to reveal them. They always appear in search results.
